About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to bring fresh, handmade goods to the community. Our goal is to create a welcoming neighborhood bakery where customers can enjoy quality breads, pastries, and cakes.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe in using quality ingredients and supporting local suppliers whenever possible. Our baked goods are prepared with care and made fresh for our customers.

Staff Highlights

Our friendly team of bakers and staff work together to create fresh baked goods and a welcoming experience for our community.
